# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Invited family and friends of Amaris's family, mostly opening the link on their phones from a shared message or invitation. Their job is quick: find out when and where the party is, and tell the hosts whether they're coming.

## Product Purpose

An event site for Amaris's first birthday ("ONE-derful"). It gives guests the event details and a way to RSVP. Success means guests find the details without asking the hosts and send their RSVP.

## Positioning

A personal, single-event site for one child's first birthday — not a generic invitation template. Its content (the child, the family's photos, the chosen theme) is what no other site can copy.

## Operating Context

- Guests arrive from a shared link, usually on mobile, often only once or twice before the event.
- RSVPs are collected through a Google Form / Sheet the hosts run; the site links to or embeds it rather than storing responses itself.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core functions: event details, RSVP via the hosts' Google Form, open-in-Maps, add-to-calendar, a countdown, and a photo gallery.
- Confirmed event (from the printed invitation, `public/photos/invitation.jpg`): **Amaris Keziah D. Benedicto's 1st birthday**, **Sunday, November 22, 2026, 6:00 PM** (Philippine time), **Jollibee Potrero Malabon, MacArthur Highway**.
- Open: the Google Form link, any RSVP deadline, and whether plus-ones or kids are invited. Until the link exists the RSVP control is shown as not yet open, never as a dead link.

## Brand Commitments

- The name/pun "ONE-derful" (repo: `amaris-onederful`).
- Theme: Cinderella fairytale, as on the printed invitation — sky and cornflower blue, gold, cream; castle, pumpkin carriage, glass slipper, roses, bows, tiara. The "ONE" in gold capitals with "derful" in blue script is the event's wordmark. Use generic fairytale motifs only; no Disney characters, logos or film imagery.
- Invitation copy that must be kept: "A little princess is turning ONE-derful", "Join us to celebrate", "We would be delighted to celebrate this special day with you!"

## Evidence on Hand

- Four studio photos of Amaris in `public/photos/` (`amaris-blue-gown`, `amaris-lilac-cry`, `amaris-pink-castle`, `amaris-blue-tulle`), carrying the photographer's watermark; use them as supplied, no separate credit line. Do not add stock or generated photos of her.
- Never invent host names, an RSVP deadline, or any other event fact not listed above.

## Product Principles

1. Details first: date, time, place and the RSVP action are reachable within the first screen on a phone.
2. Real over invented: only confirmed details, real photos, and the decided theme — placeholders, never fabrications.
3. Personal, not templated: it should feel made for Amaris, through her photos and the family's theme.
4. One-tap RSVP: hand guests to the Google Form with as little friction as possible.
